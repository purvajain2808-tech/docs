Broad, unfocused revision rarely translates into strong viva performance. By selecting the specific topics most relevant to your upcoming exam, you spend your preparation time where it counts, build deeper understanding in key areas faster, and walk into your oral exam with genuine confidence rather than surface-level familiarity across too wide a range. Viva Mate AI's topic selection tools are designed to help you do exactly that.

## Browsing Available Topics

From the **Practice** screen, open the **Topic** dropdown to browse all topics available under your chosen subject. Viva Mate AI organises its content in a three-level hierarchy:

- **Subject** — the broadest category, such as Machine Learning or Deep Learning.
- **Topic** — a major area within that subject, such as Supervised Learning or CNNs.
- **Subtopic** — a focused concept within a topic, such as Decision Trees within Supervised Learning.

Scroll through the dropdown to explore what is available, or start typing to filter the list. Selecting a topic or subtopic limits your session questions to that area, giving you a concentrated block of practice exactly where you need it.

## Choosing Based on Your Syllabus

Your university module outline is the most reliable guide to which topics deserve the most attention. Open your course syllabus alongside Viva Mate AI and cross-reference the topics listed there with the topics available in the dropdown. For each module learning outcome or lecture heading on your syllabus, find the closest matching topic in Viva Mate AI and add it to your revision plan. This ensures your practice sessions mirror what your examiner is likely to ask about, rather than covering material that may not be assessed.

## Recommended Topic Order

If you are new to a subject and unsure where to begin, follow the progression below. Each sequence moves from foundational concepts to more advanced material, so earlier topics give you the vocabulary and context you need to understand later ones.

| Subject | Recommended Progression |
|---|---|
| **Machine Learning** | Fundamentals → Supervised Learning → Unsupervised Learning → Model Evaluation |
| **Deep Learning** | Neural Network Basics → CNNs → RNNs → Transformers |
| **Data Science** | Data Wrangling → EDA → Feature Engineering → Pipelines |
| **Statistics** | Probability → Distributions → Hypothesis Testing → Regression |

Work through each stage until you are consistently scoring well before advancing to the next topic. Skipping ahead before the foundations are solid often leads to confusion with more complex material.

## Multi-Topic Sessions

When you are ready to practise across the full breadth of a subject — or want to simulate the unpredictable range of a real viva — select **All Topics** from the topic dropdown. Viva Mate AI will draw questions from every topic under your chosen subject in a randomised order. This is especially useful in the final days before your exam, when consolidating your knowledge across topics is more valuable than drilling any single area.

<Info>
  Your topic history is saved, so you can see which topics you've practised and which you've skipped. Check the **Progress** tab to identify any gaps in your coverage before your exam date.
</Info>
