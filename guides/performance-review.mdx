Consistent practice only turns into exam readiness when you pay attention to the patterns in your performance. Viva Mate AI's performance tools give you a clear, data-driven picture of where you stand across every subject and topic you have practised — so instead of guessing what to revise next, you can make deliberate decisions based on your actual results. Check your progress regularly and use what you find to keep your preparation sharp and focused right up to exam day.

## The Progress Dashboard

Open the **Progress** tab from the main navigation to access your performance dashboard. The dashboard gives you a high-level overview of your preparation at a glance, showing four key metrics:

- **Overall average score** — your mean score across every question you have answered.
- **Sessions completed** — the total number of practice sessions and Mock Vivas you have finished.
- **Questions answered** — the cumulative count of questions you have submitted across all sessions.
- **Time spent** — the total time you have invested in Viva Mate AI since you started.

These figures update automatically after every session, so your dashboard always reflects your most recent activity.

## Topic Performance Breakdown

Below the headline metrics, a bar chart displays your average score for each topic you have practised. This breakdown makes it immediately clear which topics you have mastered and which still need work. Any topic where your average score falls below **50%** is flagged with a visual indicator, signalling that it needs focused attention before your exam. Click on any bar in the chart to drill down into the individual questions and sessions that make up that topic's average.

## Score Trends

The **Score Trends** section shows a line chart of your average score over time, plotted session by session. An upward trend confirms that your preparation is working; a plateau or downward trend is a prompt to change your approach — perhaps by revisiting foundational topics, adjusting the difficulty level, or increasing session frequency. Use this chart as your preparation health check: the closer you get to your exam, the more consistently upward that line should be moving.

## Recommended Actions

Based on your weakest topics and most recent session results, Viva Mate AI surfaces a short list of **Recommended Actions** on the Progress dashboard. These suggestions might include practising a specific topic where your score has dropped, attempting a Mock Viva if you have not run one recently, or trying a higher difficulty level on a topic where you are consistently scoring above 80%. Follow these recommendations to keep your preparation targeted and efficient rather than repeating sessions where you are already performing well.

## Exporting Your Report

You can download a PDF summary of your progress directly from the **Progress** tab by clicking **Export Report**. The PDF includes your overall statistics, topic performance breakdown, and score trend chart in a clean, readable format. This is particularly useful if you want to share your preparation record with a lecturer, academic supervisor, or study mentor, or simply keep a personal record of your progress over the course of your revision period.

<Tip>
  Review your progress at least once a week and adjust which topics you practise based on your weakest areas. Regular reviews prevent you from unknowingly drifting toward topics you already find easy while neglecting the ones that need the most work.
</Tip>
