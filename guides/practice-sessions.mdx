A practice session is your everyday training ground in Viva Mate AI. Unlike a mock viva — which simulates the pressure of a real oral exam with a timer and no mid-session feedback — a practice session lets you work through questions at your own pace, receive instant feedback after every answer, and build your understanding topic by topic. Use practice sessions regularly to strengthen your knowledge before you put it to the test in a mock viva.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Practice">
    From the dashboard, click **Practice** in the left sidebar. This opens the session setup screen where you configure your question preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Subject">
    Use the **Subject** dropdown to choose one of the four available subjects: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Science, or Statistics. Your selection filters all subsequent topic and difficulty options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Topic (Optional)">
    Use the **Topic** dropdown to narrow your session to a specific area — for example, select **Neural Networks** under Deep Learning. If you prefer a broad session, leave this set to **All Topics** and Viva Mate AI will draw questions from across the subject.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Difficulty">
    Choose **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, or **Advanced** to match your current confidence level. If you are unsure, leave the setting on **Auto** and Viva Mate AI will adjust the difficulty based on your recent performance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the Question">
    Read the question carefully, then type your answer in the text box provided. Take your time — there is no timer in a practice session. When you are ready, click **Submit** to send your answer for evaluation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Feedback">
    After you submit, Viva Mate AI displays your score for that answer, highlights any knowledge gaps, and shows a model answer for comparison. Use this feedback to understand exactly where your answer fell short before moving on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue or Finish">
    Click **Next Question** to receive another question and continue building your session, or click **End Session** at any time to stop and view your full session summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Session Summary

After you end a session, Viva Mate AI displays a summary of your performance. The summary shows the total number of questions you answered, your average score across those questions, and your weakest topic from the session. Use this information to guide your next session — if a particular topic consistently appears as your weakest area, prioritise it in your next round of practice before moving on to new material.

<Tip>
  Aim for at least 10 questions per session for meaningful progress tracking. Shorter sessions produce less reliable averages, making it harder to spot genuine improvement over time.
</Tip>
