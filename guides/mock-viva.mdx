A Mock Viva is Viva Mate AI's closest simulation of a real oral examination. Unlike a practice session — where you choose your topics, set your own pace, and receive feedback after every answer — a Mock Viva is timed, covers multiple topics in a randomised order, and withholds feedback until the very end. This format forces you to rely on what you genuinely know in the moment, giving you a realistic measure of your readiness and helping you build the mental stamina a real viva demands.

## Before You Start

To get the most from a Mock Viva, make sure you have laid the right groundwork first:

- **Complete several practice sessions** — work through at least a few sessions covering the topics you expect to be examined on, so you are not encountering concepts for the first time under timed conditions.
- **Feel comfortable with at least two topics** — you should be consistently scoring well in two or more topics before running your first Mock Viva.
- **Choose a quiet environment** — find a distraction-free space where you can concentrate without interruptions for the full duration of the session. Treat the Mock Viva as seriously as you would the real thing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Mock Viva">
    From the dashboard, click **Mock Viva** in the left sidebar. This opens the Mock Viva configuration screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Session">
    Set your preferences for the session:
    - **Number of questions** — choose 5, 10, or 15 questions.
    - **Subject** — select a single subject or choose **Mixed** to draw questions from all four subjects.
    - **Difficulty** — select Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

    When you are happy with your settings, proceed to the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin the Session">
    Click **Start** to begin your Mock Viva. A countdown timer appears at the top of the screen and begins immediately. Questions are delivered one at a time in a randomised order — you will not know which topic is coming next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer Each Question">
    Read each question and type your answer in the text box provided. You have a set amount of time per question. Click **Submit** when you have finished your answer, or wait for the timer to expire and your answer will be submitted automatically. No feedback is shown between questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Session">
    After you submit your final answer, click **Finish** to end the Mock Viva and submit your full session for evaluation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Your Report">
    Viva Mate AI generates a detailed report covering every question in your session. For each question, the report shows your score, written feedback explaining where your answer succeeded or fell short, and the model answer. Use this report to identify patterns in your weaker areas and target them in your next round of practice sessions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mock Viva vs Practice Session

Use the comparison below to choose the right mode for where you are in your preparation.

| Feature | Practice Session | Mock Viva |
|---|---|---|
| Feedback | After each answer | After the session |
| Timer | No | Yes |
| Topics | You choose | Randomised |
| Purpose | Skill building | Exam simulation |

As a general rule, use practice sessions to build and consolidate knowledge, then use Mock Vivas to test how well that knowledge holds up under realistic exam pressure.

<Warning>
  During a Mock Viva, you cannot pause or revisit previous questions, just like in a real exam. Once you submit an answer and move to the next question, you cannot go back. Make sure you are fully ready before you click **Start**.
</Warning>
