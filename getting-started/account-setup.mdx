Your Viva Mate AI account is the foundation of your preparation journey. A well-configured account allows the AI engine to tailor questions to your course, year, and target subjects — making every session more relevant and effective. This page covers everything from initial sign-up to ongoing account management, so you're always in control of your experience.

## Creating Your Account

Follow these steps to create and verify your account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Sign-Up Page">
    Go to [vivamate.ai/signup](https://vivamate.ai/signup). You can sign up with an email address and password, or use Google Single Sign-On for a faster setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill In Your Details">
    Enter your **full name**, **email address**, and a **secure password** (at least eight characters, including one number and one special character). The **institution or college** field is optional at this stage, but filling it in now helps the AI tailor content to your specific programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from no-reply@vivamate.ai and click the **Confirm Email** button. If the email doesn't appear within a few minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend Verification Email** on the login page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Completing Your Profile

After verifying your email, complete your profile to get the most out of Viva Mate AI:

1. **Institution** — Enter your university or college name. This allows the platform to surface questions commonly asked in your academic community.
2. **Course Year** — Select your current year of study (Year 1 through Postgraduate / PhD). The AI adjusts question depth and expected answer quality based on this setting.
3. **Preferred Subjects** — Choose one or more subjects from the available list (for example, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, or Bayesian Statistics). Your preferred subjects appear at the top of your dashboard and seed your initial question recommendations.

Keeping your profile up to date ensures the AI engine always pitches questions at the right level. You can update any of these fields at any time from **Settings > Profile**.

## Notification Settings

Viva Mate AI can send you practice reminders so you maintain a consistent study routine:

1. Go to **Settings > Notifications**.
2. Toggle **Daily Practice Reminders** on or off. When enabled, choose your preferred reminder time from the dropdown.
3. Enable **Session Summary Emails** if you'd like a brief performance report delivered to your inbox after each session.
4. Click **Save Preferences** to apply your changes immediately.

You can disable all notifications at any time by returning to this page and toggling each option off, or by clicking **Unsubscribe** in any notification email.

## Changing Your Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Settings">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Settings**, then navigate to the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Passwords">
    Type your current password in the first field, then enter and confirm your new password in the two fields below. Your new password must be at least eight characters and include at least one number and one special character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Change">
    Click **Update Password**. You'll receive a confirmation email notifying you that your password was changed. If you did not make this change, contact support immediately at support@vivamate.ai.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your privacy is important. All practice answers, scores, and feedback are private to your account — they are never shared with your institution, instructors, or other users. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from **Settings > Account > Delete Account**.
</Note>
