A viva voce is an oral examination in which you defend your knowledge in real time, answering questions posed directly by an examiner. Unlike written exams, a viva demands fluency, depth, and the ability to think on your feet — with no notes to fall back on. Viva Mate AI is built specifically for this challenge. It simulates the pressure and unpredictability of a real viva, guides you through structured practice, and gives you the kind of precise, immediate feedback that would otherwise only come from a tutor or examiner.

## The Problem with Traditional Viva Prep

Most students prepare for viva exams the same way they prepare for written ones: re-reading notes, highlighting textbooks, and hoping the right questions come up. That approach leaves critical gaps.

- **No spoken practice.** Reading silently does not prepare you to articulate ideas clearly under pressure. The ability to explain a concept out loud is a separate skill that requires deliberate repetition.
- **No exposure to unexpected questions.** Studying only what you expect to be asked means you are unprepared the moment an examiner pushes beyond the obvious topics.
- **No feedback on answer quality.** Without a tutor to evaluate your responses, you have no way of knowing whether your answers are thorough, accurate, or missing key concepts entirely.

These gaps mean many students enter their viva under-prepared — not because they lack knowledge, but because they have never practised using it in exam conditions.

## How Viva Mate AI Solves This

Viva Mate AI replaces passive revision with active, exam-style practice. Here is what it does for you:

- **Generates realistic questions.** The AI draws from curated syllabi to produce questions that match the depth and style of real viva exams — from foundational recall through to applied reasoning.
- **Evaluates your answers instantly.** After you submit a response, the evaluation engine compares it to a reference answer using semantic analysis, checks for coverage of key concepts, and produces a scored assessment.
- **Gives structured, actionable feedback.** Every answer you submit returns a numerical score, a breakdown of what you missed, a model answer you can study, and targeted tips for improvement.
- **Tracks your progress over time.** Your scores are logged across sessions so you can see which topics are improving and which still need work before exam day.

The result is a preparation experience that mirrors the real thing — giving you the practice repetitions and honest feedback you need to walk into your viva with confidence.

## Who Is It For?

Viva Mate AI is designed for undergraduate and postgraduate students preparing for departmental or university viva examinations in:

- **Artificial Intelligence** — including AI theory, ethics, and applied AI systems
- **Machine Learning** — supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning
- **Data Science** — data pipelines, analysis methods, visualisation, and interpretation
- **Related programmes** — Computer Science, Statistics, Cognitive Science, and any course with an AI or data component

Whether you are preparing for a final-year project viva, a module oral assessment, or a postgraduate thesis defence, Viva Mate AI adapts to your level and subject area.
