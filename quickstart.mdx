This guide walks you through everything you need to do to go from zero to your first scored practice session. By the end, you'll have a Viva Mate AI account, a subject selected, and a clear picture of how the feedback system works — all in five minutes or less. No prior setup is required.

## Your First Session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [vivamate.ai/signup](https://vivamate.ai/signup) and fill in your name, email address, and a secure password. Once you submit the form, check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account. The email typically arrives within one minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Subject">
    After logging in, you'll land on your personalised dashboard. Select one of the available subjects — **Machine Learning**, **Deep Learning**, **Data Science**, or **Statistics** — by clicking its tile. You can change your subject at any time from the dashboard, so don't worry about making the perfect choice right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a Practice Session">
    Click the **Start Practice** button on your chosen subject tile. Viva Mate AI will immediately present your first question. Type your answer into the text field, or click the microphone icon to respond verbally. Take your time — there is no time limit during standard practice sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Your Feedback">
    After you submit your answer, the feedback panel opens automatically. You'll see your **score out of 10**, a **model answer** written at the level expected in a real viva, and **personalised improvement tips** highlighting exactly what you missed or could articulate more clearly. Read through the feedback carefully before moving to the next question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Your Progress">
    Visit the **Progress** tab in the top navigation bar to view your performance over time. The dashboard displays your average score per topic, your session history, and a breakdown of your strongest and weakest subject areas. Return here after every session to see how you're improving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Once you feel comfortable with individual practice questions, try a **Mock Viva** session. Mock Vivas simulate a complete oral examination — with follow-up questions and time pressure — giving you the closest possible experience to the real thing before it matters.
</Tip>
