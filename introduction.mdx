## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Create your free Viva Mate AI account at [vivamate.ai/signup](https://vivamate.ai/signup). All you need is an email address and a password — you can be ready in under two minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Topic">
    Select from a wide range of AI and Data Science subjects — including Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Statistics, and Data Engineering — so every question is relevant to what you're actually being examined on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer AI Questions">
    Respond to AI-generated questions by typing or speaking your answers. The questions adapt to your performance, progressively challenging you as your understanding grows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Your Feedback">
    After each answer, receive an instant score, a detailed model answer, and targeted improvement tips. Use this feedback to close knowledge gaps and sharpen your explanations before the real thing.
  </Step>
</Steps>
