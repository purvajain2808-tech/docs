## Getting Started

<Accordion title="Is Viva Mate AI free to use?">
  Yes. Viva Mate AI offers a free tier that gives you access to a limited number of questions each day, so you can explore the platform before committing to a plan. Upgrading to a premium subscription unlocks unlimited practice sessions and mock vivas, as well as advanced analytics on your performance over time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to create an account?">
  Yes, an account is required to use Viva Mate AI. Your account is what allows the platform to track your progress, save your session history, and personalise question difficulty based on your past performance. Creating an account is free and takes less than a minute.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What subjects does Viva Mate AI cover?">
  Viva Mate AI currently covers four core subject areas:

  - **Machine Learning** — supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation, and more
  - **Deep Learning** — neural network architectures, training techniques, and applications
  - **Data Science** — data wrangling, exploratory analysis, and pipeline design
  - **Statistics** — probability, hypothesis testing, regression, and Bayesian methods

  We are continuously expanding our question bank, so check back regularly for new topics and subject areas.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is Viva Mate AI suitable for both undergraduates and postgraduates?">
  Yes. Viva Mate AI is designed to support students at every level. When you start a session, you can select a difficulty level — from **Foundational** through to **Advanced** — so whether you are studying for your first-year exams or preparing for a postgraduate viva voce, the platform adjusts to match your needs.
</Accordion>

## Practice Sessions

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a Practice Session and a Mock Viva?">
  The two modes serve different purposes in your preparation:

  - **Practice Session** — you receive detailed AI feedback immediately after each answer, making it ideal for learning and identifying gaps in your knowledge as you go.
  - **Mock Viva** — the platform simulates real exam conditions. You answer all questions without interruption and receive a full feedback report only at the end of the session, just as you would in an actual viva.

  We recommend using practice sessions to build confidence before attempting a mock viva.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I choose which topics to practise?">
  Yes. From the **Practice** screen, use the filters at the top to select your preferred subject, topic within that subject, and difficulty level. You can also leave any filter set to **Any** if you would like a mixed session that spans multiple topics.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many questions are in a session?">
  For practice sessions, you can set the session length to anywhere between **5 and 30 questions** using the slider on the session setup screen. Mock Vivas use a fixed length determined by the selected exam profile. You can adjust this at any time before the session begins.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I pause a session?">
  **Practice sessions** can be paused at any time by clicking the **Pause** button in the session toolbar. Your progress is saved automatically, and you can resume from where you left off.

  **Mock Vivas** cannot be paused once started, as pausing would break the exam simulation. If you close a mock viva early, it will be marked as incomplete and will not appear in your scored history. Make sure you have enough uninterrupted time before you begin.
</Accordion>

## Scoring & Feedback

<Accordion title="How are my answers scored?">
  After you submit an answer, Viva Mate AI's evaluation engine scores it on a scale of **0 to 10**. The score reflects three dimensions:

  1. **Concept coverage** — did you address the key ideas the question is testing?
  2. **Accuracy** — is what you said technically correct?
  3. **Clarity** — is your explanation well-structured and easy to follow?

  Each session's feedback screen breaks down your score across these dimensions and shows you a model answer so you can see exactly what a strong response looks like.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I appeal or dispute a score?">
  Scores are generated automatically by the AI evaluation engine, and there is no manual review process for individual question scores. We encourage you to read the detailed feedback and model answer provided — these will help you understand how your response was assessed and what to include next time.

  If you believe a score is the result of a technical error (for example, your answer was not recorded correctly), please contact [support@vivamate.ai](mailto:support@vivamate.ai) with the session ID and we will investigate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are my answers and scores private?">
  Yes. All of your answers, scores, and session data are private to your account and are never shared with other users, institutions, or third parties. You can review and delete your data at any time from **Settings > Privacy**. If you have any questions about data handling, contact us at support@vivamate.ai.
</Accordion>

## Account & Billing

<Accordion title="How do I upgrade to premium?">
  To upgrade your account, follow these steps:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Settings">
      Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner and select **Settings** from the dropdown menu.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Go to Subscription">
      Select the **Subscription** tab in the left-hand navigation.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose a plan">
      Review the available plans and click **Upgrade** next to the one that suits you. You will be taken to a secure checkout page to complete payment.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  Premium access is activated immediately after a successful payment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription?">
  Yes, you can cancel your premium subscription at any time with no cancellation fee. Go to **Settings > Subscription** and click **Cancel Plan**. You will retain full premium access until the end of your current billing period, after which your account will revert to the free tier. You will not be charged again after cancellation.
</Accordion>
