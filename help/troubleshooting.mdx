If something is not working as expected, you are in the right place. Most problems with Viva Mate AI can be resolved quickly with the steps below, without needing to contact anyone. Work through the relevant section for your issue, and if the problem persists after following all the steps, our support team is ready to help — details at the bottom of this page.

## Login & Account Issues

<Accordion title="I can't log in">
  If you are unable to sign in to your account, try the following steps in order:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your email and password">
      Make sure you are using the email address you registered with and that Caps Lock is not accidentally enabled. Passwords are case-sensitive.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Reset your password">
      On the login page, click **Forgot password?** and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it does not appear in your inbox.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Clear your browser cache">
      Outdated cached data can sometimes prevent the login page from loading correctly. Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then reload the page and try again.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Try a different browser or device">
      If the issue continues, try logging in using a different browser (for example, Chrome, Firefox, or Safari) or a different device to rule out a local browser problem.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I didn't receive my verification email">
  Verification emails are usually delivered within a minute or two of registration. If yours has not arrived:

  1. **Check your spam or junk folder** — automated emails from new services are sometimes filtered automatically.
  2. **Wait up to 5 minutes** — delivery can occasionally be delayed due to mail server processing times.
  3. **Resend the verification email** — return to the login page and click **Resend verification email**. Enter your registered email address and a new link will be sent.

  <Tip>
    Add **no-reply@vivamate.ai** to your email contacts or safe-sender list to prevent future emails from being filtered.
  </Tip>

  If you have tried all of the above and still have not received the email, contact [support@vivamate.ai](mailto:support@vivamate.ai) with your registered email address and we will verify your account manually.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My account is locked">
  For your security, Viva Mate AI automatically locks an account after **5 consecutive failed login attempts**. This prevents unauthorised access to your account.

  To unlock your account:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Request a password reset">
      Go to the login page and click **Forgot password?**. Enter your registered email address to receive a reset link.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Set a new password">
      Open the reset link from your email and choose a new, strong password. Using a password manager is a good way to avoid this issue in the future.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Log in with your new password">
      Return to the login page and sign in with your new credentials. Your account will be automatically unlocked upon a successful login.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Warning>
    If you did not make those login attempts yourself, change your password immediately and contact support, as your account credentials may have been compromised.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

## Session & Question Issues

<Accordion title="A session won't start">
  If you click **Start Session** and nothing happens, or the session fails to load, try the following:

  1. **Check your internet connection** — Viva Mate AI requires a stable connection to load questions and submit answers. Run a quick speed test or try loading another website to confirm your connection is working.
  2. **Try a different browser** — some browser configurations can interfere with session loading. Switch to an up-to-date version of Chrome, Firefox, or Safari.
  3. **Disable browser extensions** — ad blockers, script blockers, and privacy extensions can sometimes prevent the session from initialising. Temporarily disable your extensions and try again.
  4. **Refresh the page** — a simple page refresh resolves the issue in many cases.

  <Info>
    If sessions consistently fail to start, check the [Viva Mate AI status page](#) to see if there is a known service disruption.
  </Info>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My answer was not submitted">
  After you submit an answer, you should see a **green confirmation tick** appear briefly on the screen before the next question or feedback panel loads. This tick confirms that your answer has been received and recorded.

  If you do not see the confirmation tick:

  - **Do not navigate away from the page** — doing so may cause your answer to be lost.
  - **Check your internet connection**, then click the **Submit** button again.
  - If the tick still does not appear, refresh the page. Your session should resume from your last confirmed submission.

  <Warning>
    If you repeatedly experience submission failures during a session, end the session and check your connection before starting a new one. Submitting an answer on a broken connection may result in it being recorded as blank.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I'm seeing repeated questions">
  If you notice the same questions appearing across multiple sessions, this is expected behaviour rather than a bug. When you have attempted all available questions for a given topic and difficulty level, the question bank cycles back to the beginning.

  To get more variety, try the following:

  - **Select a different topic** within the same subject.
  - **Change the difficulty level** — questions at each level are stored independently.
  - **Switch subjects** — for example, move from Machine Learning to Statistics for a fresh set of questions.

  <Tip>
    We add new questions to the bank regularly. Revisiting topics after a few weeks is also a great way to reinforce what you have learned.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The session timer ran out before I could answer">
  If the timer for a question expires before you submit an answer, the platform automatically submits a blank response, which is scored **0** for that question. This is intentional — it mirrors the time pressure of a real viva.

  To avoid this in future sessions:

  - Use **practice sessions** (which have no timer) to build familiarity with common question types before moving to timed modes.
  - Start with a **lower difficulty level** to build speed and confidence, then work your way up.
  - Review the model answers in your feedback report to understand the key points you need to cover, so you can respond more efficiently next time.
</Accordion>

## Progress & Data Issues

<Accordion title="My progress is not showing">
  Your progress dashboard updates automatically after each **completed session**. If you ended a session early or closed the browser before the final summary screen, that session may not have been recorded.

  If you have completed sessions and your progress still looks incorrect:

  1. **Refresh the page** — the dashboard sometimes requires a manual refresh to display the latest data.
  2. **Log out and log back in** — this forces the app to reload your account data from the server.
  3. **Wait a few minutes** — in rare cases, there can be a short delay before new session data is reflected in the dashboard.

  If your progress remains missing after trying the above, contact support with the approximate date and time of the affected sessions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't download my progress report">
  Progress reports are available as PDF downloads from the **Progress** tab in your dashboard. If the download is not working:

  - **Ensure you have completed at least one full session** — the report cannot be generated if there is no session data associated with your account.
  - **Try a different browser** — PDF generation relies on browser-level download handling, which can be blocked by certain browser settings or extensions. Chrome and Firefox generally work most reliably.
  - **Check your downloads folder** — the file may have downloaded silently without a visible notification, depending on your browser settings.
  - **Disable pop-up blockers** — some browsers block the PDF download prompt as a pop-up. Temporarily disable your pop-up blocker and try again.

  <Info>
    Premium users can also access a detailed breakdown report with topic-level analysis. If you are on the free tier, your report will include session summaries only.
  </Info>
</Accordion>
